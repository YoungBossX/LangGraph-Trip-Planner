# Destination Image Attributions

Each city is delivered as two responsive processed WebP derivatives: `city.webp` at 1600 x 1200 and `city-800.webp` at 800 x 600. Derivatives were resized with Lanczos resampling, re-encoded as WebP at quality 76 with method 6, and saved without retained EXIF metadata.
